# Founder Proof Runbook

This is the simple June 1 proof flow.

Use the June 1 proof workspace.

Goal:
Load the three proof files and watch ShowTELA build the working view.

What you need:
- The Anchor Directory file
- The Calendar file
- The Rider file
- A signed-in ShowTELA session

## Start Here

1. Open ShowTELA.
2. Open the June 1 proof workspace.
3. Make sure the screen looks mostly empty except for the main controls.

What you should see:
- The main ShowTELA home screen
- An `Add continuity` button

## Step 1: Load The Anchor Directory

1. Tap `Add continuity`.
2. Tap `Upload Files`.
3. Choose the Anchor Directory file.
4. Tap `Add Continuity`.

What you expect:
- The team should appear.
- The page should stop feeling empty.

What you should receive:
- The people rail fills in with names.
- The operations area starts to populate.
- The page stays stable after the upload closes.

## Step 2: Load The Calendar

1. Tap `Add continuity` again.
2. Tap `Upload Files`.
3. Choose the Calendar file.
4. Tap `Add Continuity`.

What you expect:
- The schedule should appear.
- The calendar should start feeling alive.

What you should receive:
- The week view fills in.
- The calendar tab becomes useful.
- More operations appear.

## Step 3: Load The Rider

1. Tap `Add continuity` again.
2. Tap `Upload Files`.
3. Choose the Rider file.
4. Tap `Add Continuity`.

What you expect:
- The support departments should join the working picture.

What you should receive:
- The feed grows.
- The operations set feels complete.
- The page still loads cleanly and stays responsive.

## Final Result

At the end of the full proof flow, you should have:
- 18 people
- 18 operations
- 32 calendar items
- 52 feed items

What that means in plain language:
- The team is there.
- The schedule is there.
- The rider is there.
- The workspace looks complete.

## If Something Looks Wrong

If the screen still looks empty:
- Refresh once.
- Reopen the same workspace.

If an upload closes but nothing changes:
- Wait a moment.
- Refresh once.
- Check the same workspace again.

If the wrong workspace opens:
- Go back to the June 1 proof workspace and continue there.

## What Success Looks Like

Success is:
- The page opens cleanly.
- The uploads finish without a crash.
- The people appear.
- The schedule appears.
- The departments appear.
- The workspace feels ready to show.
