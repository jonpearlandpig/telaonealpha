# TELAOne Design System Constitution

## Core Intent
The design system exists to deliver continuity-first cognition with operational calm and cinematic hierarchy. Every surface must reduce cognitive thrash and increase confident action.

## Color System
- **Navy** `#0D1B2A` as foundational depth and control plane.
- **Cream** `#EAE0D2` as reading field and calm contrast layer.
- **Gold** `#C4973A` as authority accent and decision emphasis.

Use color to signal hierarchy, not decoration.

## Typography Hierarchy
- Headline layers establish cinematic narrative order.
- Body layers prioritize legibility and continuity scanning.
- Utility labels remain sparse, precise, and subordinate.
- Typographic rhythm must reinforce operational calm.

## Spacing Philosophy
- Whitespace is luxury.
- Editorial spacing takes precedence over compact density.
- Vertical rhythm must preserve cognitive breathing room.
- Layout composition should feel intentional, not compressed.

## Tactile Interaction Doctrine
- Controls must feel touch-native and physically plausible.
- Tap surfaces are large, calm, and deliberate.
- Feedback should be immediate but restrained.
- Interaction should preserve continuity visibility at all times.

## Cinematic Hierarchy
- Primary intention appears first, with progressive disclosure below.
- Motion and contrast reinforce narrative significance.
- Visual layering should guide attention with minimum noise.

## Motion Restraint
- Motion must be deliberate, subtle, and hierarchy-serving.
- Avoid spectacle; favor clarity.
- Transition timing should support orientation and continuity.

## Operational Calm
- Interfaces should feel composed under load.
- Reduce simultaneous emphasis points.
- Preserve confidence through consistency, lineage cues, and calm pacing.

## Explicit Prohibitions
- No startup gradients.
- No clutter.
- No generic SaaS layouts.
- No dashboard sprawl.
- No tiny tap targets.
