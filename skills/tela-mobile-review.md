# TELA Mobile Review Skill

## Evaluation Criteria
- mobile ergonomics
- large tap surfaces
- thumb-zone prioritization
- swipe and snap quality
- momentum behavior
- calm hierarchy at 393px
- continuity visibility on handheld surfaces

## Rejection Criteria
- desktop-first composition
- tap targets below ergonomic standards
- cramped spacing harming cognition
- gesture conflicts or jitter

## Completion Criteria
- mobile-first interaction is verified
- gesture behaviors are stable and tactile
- hierarchy remains calm and legible
- continuity cues remain visible throughout core flows

## Regression Warnings
- gradual return of desktop affordances
- reduced spacing leading to accidental taps
- hidden continuity cues on smaller screens
