# TELA Design Review Skill

## Evaluation Criteria
- cinematic quality
- operational calm
- tactile hierarchy
- modular consistency
- motion hierarchy
- interaction restraint
- continuity visibility
- artifact materialization clarity

## Rejection Criteria
- visual clutter or dashboard sprawl
- tiny tap targets
- non-cinematic hierarchy
- excessive motion or gimmick animations
- broken modular consistency

## Completion Criteria
- cinematic hierarchy is clear
- operational calm is preserved
- tactile hierarchy is coherent
- motion is restrained and meaningful
- artifact materialization is visible and stable

## Regression Warnings
- creeping density reducing readability
- animation escalation over time
- inconsistency between related surfaces
