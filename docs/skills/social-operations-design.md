# Social Operations Design Skill

## Purpose

Teach Codex that ShowTELA is relational operational software, not sterile enterprise tooling.

## Operational Presence Should Feel

- human
- relational
- ambient
- socially aware
- team-aware
- trust-building

## Prioritize

- identity visibility
- continuity presence
- relationship awareness
- team fluency
- recent human activity
- clear ownership

## Avoid

- sterile enterprise aesthetics
- faceless workflow systems
- generic avatars without context
- unclear responsibility

## Operating Rule

Operational systems should feel socially alive without drifting into chat or notification theater.
