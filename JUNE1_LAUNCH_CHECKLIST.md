# June 1 Launch Checklist

## Before Juan Arrives

- Open ShowTELA.
- Confirm you are signed in.
- Confirm the June 1 proof workspace opens.
- Keep the three proof files easy to reach.
- Close anything unrelated.

## Workspace Setup

- Open the June 1 proof workspace.
- Make sure the page opens cleanly.
- If the page looks old, refresh once.
- Stay on the home screen.

## Proof Files

- Anchor Directory
- Calendar
- Rider

Load them in that order.

## Demo Flow

1. Tap `Add continuity`.
2. Tap `Upload Files`.
3. Load the Anchor Directory.
4. Tap `Add Continuity`.
5. Repeat the same steps for the Calendar.
6. Repeat the same steps for the Rider.
7. Pause on the home screen.
8. Show the people row.
9. Show the operations area.
10. Show the calendar tab.

## Expected Results

- The page stays open and calm.
- The team appears.
- The schedule appears.
- The departments appear.
- The workspace feels complete.

Target final picture:
- 18 people
- 18 operations
- 32 calendar items
- 52 feed items

## Recovery Plan

If the page looks empty:
- Refresh once.
- Reopen the same workspace.

If an upload seems to do nothing:
- Wait a moment.
- Refresh once.
- Check the same workspace again.

If the wrong place opens:
- Return to the June 1 proof workspace.

If the page stops looking right:
- Refresh once before doing anything else.

## Known Limitations

- The proof depends on the three files being loaded into the right workspace.
- The order matters: Anchor Directory first, then Calendar, then Rider.
- The proof is meant to show this exact June 1 path, not every possible path.

## Post Demo Actions

- Leave the June 1 proof workspace open.
- Do not load unrelated files into it.
- Note anything surprising while it is still fresh.
- Save the exact workspace link that was used for the demonstration.
